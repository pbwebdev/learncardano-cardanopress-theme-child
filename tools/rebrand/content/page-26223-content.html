Our Metrics

When you book an interview with Learn Crypto, here's what's included:

  • Edited for pacing and engagement; we handle all post-production

Written Content

  • Blog post published on Learn Crypto covering key discussion points, with links to your project and socials
  • SEO-optimised for crypto and Cardano ecosystem discovery

Social & Promotion

  • Dedicated X posts for the full interview announcement
  • Individual X posts for each short-form clip (minimum 3 posts)
  • Cross-posted to our Instagram and TikTok audiences
  • Mentioned in our weekly newsletter ([link to newsletter])

Podcast Distribution

  • Full audio episode distributed to Spotify and major podcast platforms (following week after YouTube release)
  • Interview remains live across all platforms indefinitely

Timeline

  • Shorts and clips: Distributed over 2 weeks post-release across platforms
  • YouTube release: [X days after recording]
  • Podcast/Spotify release: 7 days after YouTube
  • Blog post: Published alongside YouTube release

What We Do Not Cover or Mention

  • Token launches, token sales, or pre-sale announcements of any kind
  • Financial advice or price speculation (buying, selling, or investment recommendations)
  • Projects in early development stages without mainnet deployment or demonstrable traction
  • Sponsored content or paid promotional placements
  • Projects that lack transparency about team, funding, or technical implementation
  • Pump-and-dump schemes, Ponzi structures, or other financially exploitative models
  • Unvetted or anonymous team members without verifiable track records
  • Rebrand attempts of failed projects under new names

Why These Standards?

Learn Crypto exists to surface real innovation across crypto, with Cardano and Midnight at its core. Our audience trusts us to filter signal from noise. Maintaining these standards protects that trust and ensures every interview represents genuine value, not marketing spend.

How We Vet Projects

All submissions go through our editorial review process. We assess projects based on: technical merit, team credibility, ecosystem contribution, and alignment with Cardano/Midnight values. We prioritise projects that are shipping, not talking. If your project doesn't fit, we'll let you know within 1–2 weeks with transparent feedback.

Recording & Logistics

  • Interviews are 30–60 minutes
  • Conducted via Riverside (we handle setup and recording)
  • You'll receive a copy of the raw video for your own use
  • We handle all editing, thumbnails, and publishing

Standard Interviews (Free)

Regular interviews are added to our release queue at no cost. Our vetting process typically takes 1–2 weeks. You can view our upcoming release schedule on our Scheduled Release Google Calendar (subject to change). We publish roughly 3 free interviews a month.

Support us while you wait: If you'd like to support the channel, consider buying us a coffee or becoming a member on Buy Me a Coffee.

Interviews are exclusive to Learn Crypto for 7 days after the YouTube release. After that, you're welcome to republish on your own channels or platforms.
